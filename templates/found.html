Advice for Finders

  • Keep secret details: Don't disclose screen unlock codes, full card numbers, or pouch contents so the real owner can verify them.
  • Clear location: Mention the room or bench where you discovered the item.
  • Safe Handover: Always verify student ID before handing the item over.
